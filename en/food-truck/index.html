FAQ

Common questions.

How much does it cost to book the truck?
Within the Minsk region the visit is free from 150 meals. Other regions are priced individually.
Do you need electricity?
No, the truck has its own generator and water for 8 hours.
Can guests pay by card?
Yes: cards, phone payments and app coupons.
How far ahead should I book?
In summer 3–4 weeks ahead, otherwise a week is enough.
