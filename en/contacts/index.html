FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Where do your ingredients come from?
Most come from Belarusian farms and producers: potatoes, flour, dairy, eggs and beef. Our own bakery makes the buns, sauces and pies every night.
Until when is breakfast served?
Until 10:00 in every restaurant. Lunch combos run from 12:00 to 16:00, the night menu starts at 23:00 in 24/7 locations.
How fast is delivery?
About 28 minutes on average. The app shows the courier on the map so you always know when to expect the bag.
Something is missing from my order. What now?
Message us in the app chat or through the form on this page and we’ll refund you or bring what’s missing.
Can I book a kitchen tour?
Yes. Tours are free on weekends for families and school groups. Choose “Kitchen tour” in the form above.
Is there something for allergies or diets?
Every dish on the menu lists its allergens and nutrition. There is a plant-based patty, and most burgers can be ordered without the bun.
How does Bunly Club work?
It’s an in-app subscription: free delivery over 15 BYN, a free daily drink and double points. Cancel any time.
Can we hold a kids’ party with you?
Yes, in restaurants with a kids area. Write to us and the manager will find a time.
Do you take cards and phone payments?
Yes, everywhere: restaurants, drive-thru, couriers and the food truck.
How do I become a supplier?
Choose “Partnership” in the form and tell us about your farm or product.
