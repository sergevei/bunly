About

We feed people the way we would at home.

Bunly started with one restaurant in Minsk and a simple idea: fast food can be local, honest and really tasty. Here is how it works.

Field to tray

Four steps to your burger.

Local farms

Potatoes, flour, dairy, eggs and beef come from 60 Belarusian farms, most of them within a day’s drive.

85%local ingredients

Our own bakery

Every night in Minsk we bake buns and pies and deliver them to every restaurant by morning.

120 000buns a night

Quality lab

Every batch is checked on 14 points, from bun colour to patty weight and storage temperature.

14checks per batch

An open kitchen

Your burger is built in front of you in about two minutes. On weekends you can tour the kitchen.

2 minto build a burger

Grown and made with 60+ local partners

Story

How we grew.

  1. 2019

    First restaurant on Nemiga

    Twelve tables, one grill and a queue round the corner on day one.

  2. 2020

    Delivery and the app

    We launched couriers and an app with live order tracking.

  3. 2021

    Our own bakery

    We stopped buying buns and started baking our own.

  4. 2022

    Drive-thru and 24/7

    The first drive-thru restaurants and the night menu.

  5. 2023

    The food truck

    Bunly on wheels started touring towns without a restaurant.

  6. 2025

    Bunly Club

    A subscription with free delivery and a daily drink.

  7. 2026

    31 restaurants

    From Brest to Vitebsk, and we are just getting started.

Values

What we believe.

01

Local means fresh

The shorter the trip from the field, the better the taste and the shorter the ingredient list.

02

Open means honest

The kitchen is visible from the dining room; every dish lists its ingredients and allergens.

03

Fast, never rushed

Two minutes per burger is about a well-run kitchen, not cutting corners.

Come and tour the kitchen.

Free on weekends for families and school groups: the grill, the fryers and a burger you build yourself.
